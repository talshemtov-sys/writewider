Write Wider – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Write Wider ("the app") is an accessibility app for Android. When your keyboard opens, it shows the text you are typing in a large pane above the keyboard, so it is easier to read and edit.

The short version

  • Everything the app does happens on your phone.
  • The app has no internet access. It cannot send anything anywhere.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share anything you type.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no account.

What the app reads, and why

To show your text in the large pane, the app uses Android's Accessibility service. With it, the app:

  • notices when your keyboard opens and closes;
  • reads the text of the box you are typing in, only to show it in the pane;
  • moves the cursor, selects, cuts, copies and pastes in that box, only when you touch the pane.

The app never reads password fields. It never types or sends messages for you; you always tap Send yourself.

What is stored on your phone

The app keeps a few settings on your phone only: the size and position of the pane, your text size, how the pane opens, and the list of apps where you chose to keep it closed. If the app is closed unexpectedly while you are typing, it may keep a copy of your unsent text on your phone so it is not lost; this copy is deleted once the text is safely back in the app you were typing in.

The app also keeps a diagnostic log on your phone, to help fix problems. It records technical events, such as when the keyboard opened, which app it opened in, the type and length of the text box, and the time. It never contains the text you type. The log is sent to no one: only if you choose to share it yourself, from the app's testing tools or by sending feedback, does it leave your phone, and then only to the person you send it to.

Feedback you choose to send. The app has a "Send feedback" screen. What you write or say there and, unless you switch it off, the technical details (phone model and Android version, your keyboard, the app you were writing in and the type of its text box, other accessibility apps that are on, the app's settings and its diagnostic log; never what you type) go into an email in your own email app. If you open feedback with the button on the pane, the app also takes one picture of your screen at that moment, so we can see the problem; the feedback screen shows it, and you can remove it before sending. The app takes no other screenshots. You see the whole email, can change it, and only you send it. The app itself sends nothing: it has no internet access.

None of this leaves your phone on its own. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Permissions

  • Accessibility service: described above.
  • List of apps with an icon: only to show you the list where you choose apps to keep the pane closed in. The list is not stored or sent anywhere.

The app does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
